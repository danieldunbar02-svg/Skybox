Each click on Place Box creates a new independent box. The fields above apply only to new boxes. Drag inside a box to move it. Select a box to reveal the white rotation handle above it, then drag the handle to rotate. The angle in degrees appears beside the handle. Click the map to deselect. Measure: the first click drops a pin and a dashed preview follows your cursor; the second click finishes the measurement. Marker: choose a shape visually, place it once, then use the separate corner handles to resize width and height, or the top handle to rotate. Name the selected shape in its editor. Undo/redo tracks edits. Your workspace saves automatically; use Save project to keep a named copy.
